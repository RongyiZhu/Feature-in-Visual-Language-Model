\subsection{Understanding Neural Representations}

Neural representations are commonly studied through input-level visualization and
attribution~\cite{ZeilerFergus2014,SimonyanEtAl2014,OlahEtAl2017,SelvarajuEtAl2017},
probing and its controls~\cite{AlainBengio2017,Belinkov2022,HewittLiang2019}, and
concept-level interpretation~\cite{BauEtAl2017,KimEtAl2018,GhorbaniEtAl2019}.
Concept methods range from supervised and post-hoc bottlenecks to label-free
decompositions and contrastive directions~\cite{KohEtAl2020,YuksekgonulEtAl2023,
OikarinenEtAl2023LF,CunninghamEtAl2023,ZouEtAl2023}.  We similarly estimate
directions, but jointly recover class-relative axes from controlled visual contrasts
and test their cross-domain transfer.

\subsection{Vision--Language Models}

VLMs have progressed from region-based cross-modal encoders to patch-based fusion and
alignment~\cite{LuEtAl2019ViLBERT,TanBansal2019LXMERT,ChenEtAl2020UNITER,
KimEtAl2021ViLT,LiEtAl2021ALBEF}, large-scale contrastive
pretraining~\cite{RadfordEtAl2021,JiaEtAl2021}, and generative models coupling vision
encoders with instruction-tuned language models~\cite{AlayracEtAl2022,
LiEtAl2023BLIP2,LiuEtAl2023LLaVA,BaiEtAl2023QwenVL,WangEtAl2024Qwen2VL,
BaiEtAl2025Qwen25VL}.  Since architecture and training choices materially affect
behavior~\cite{KaramchetiEtAl2024}, internal findings may not transfer across models.

VLM interpretation studies either analyze global embeddings through neurons,
text-grounded decomposition, and sparse concepts~\cite{GohEtAl2021,OikarinenWeng2023,
GandelsmanEtAl2024,BhallaEtAl2024,ZaigrajewEtAl2025,PapadimitriouEtAl2025,
PachEtAl2025}, or trace visual information through decoder layers, interventions, and
failure modes~\cite{NeoEtAl2025,KrojerEtAl2026,LiEtAl2025CausalTracing,TongEtAl2024,
ChenEtAl2024FastV,JiangEtAl2024,SongEtAl2026}.  Unlike largely global or descriptive
analyses, we recover multiple class-relative directions from matched visual
replacements and test transfer to natural images without fitted probes or score-time
masks.
